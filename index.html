Our Tradition

Our Path

The Traditional Journey

01

The Courtship

What began as a simple connection grew into a love rooted in respect, friendship, and shared purpose. With the blessing of their families, Moabi and Lebo choose to walk life's journey together — honouring both love and culture.

02

Magadi / Lobola

In the presence of elders and family, two households came together in dignity, dialogue, and mutual respect. This sacred tradition marks not only the union of two people, but the joining of two families and their histories.

03

The Gift Exchange

Gifts shared with gratitude and joy — symbols of appreciation, unity, and the weaving together of two homes. Through laughter, celebration, and culture, bonds between families will be strengthened for generations.

04

The Wedding Day

On 26 September 2026, Moabi and Lebo stand before those they love most to celebrate a journey guided by faith, family, and love. Two hearts become one future — rich in culture, abundant in joy.
